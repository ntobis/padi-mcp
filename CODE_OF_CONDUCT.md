# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct. The full text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: everyone taking part in this project's spaces is expected to be
respectful, considerate, and welcoming, and to assume good faith. Conduct that
makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome has no place here.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be reported privately to the project maintainer at
**nicolas.tobis@me.com**. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
The maintainer may take any action they consider appropriate, up to and
including removing contributions or barring a participant from the project's
spaces.
